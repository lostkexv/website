What services does Keshav Associates offer?+

Services include LIC new policy assistance and policy corrections, car and two-wheeler insurance enquiries, passport applications, Voter Card, PAN Card and Aadhaar-related assistance, online form filling, money transfer, AEPS cash withdrawal, railway, bus and air ticket bookings, photostat, instant passport-size photos, stationery, printouts, lamination and book binding.

Where is Keshav Associates located?+

We are at 22-C, Jagdish Colony, Rajpura Road, Patiala, Punjab, India. Use the Get Directions link on this website to open the location in Google Maps.

What are the business hours?+

Keshav Associates is open every day, Monday through Sunday, from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

How can I contact Keshav Associates?+

Call 0175-291-0784 or email keshavassociates4@gmail.com. You can also visit the business during opening hours.
